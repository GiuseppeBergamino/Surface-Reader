Prompt: Which control should I use to increase the noise level? Describe where it is and what it does.

# Surface Reader:
## R1:
Use the NOISE knob.

It is in the VCO section of the synthesizer controls. From your playing position, think of the upper-left synth area: the NOISE knob is a rotary knob near the oscillator controls, below the OCTAVE selector area and close to the WAVE select switch. It is control 3 in the manual’s synthesizer panel diagram.

What it does: the manual says the NOISE knob adjusts the level of white noise mixed with the VCO output. Turning it up adds more white noise into the synth sound before it continues through the filter and amp.

I cannot know its current position from the manual or static panel photo alone.
