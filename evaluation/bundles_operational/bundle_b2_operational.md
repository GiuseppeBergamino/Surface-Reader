Prompt ID: b2

Prompt: Which control should I use to record a sequence? Describe where it is and how to use it.

# Surface Reader:
## R1:
Use the REC button in the Sequencer section. It records the synth sequence when you play the ribbon keyboard. The manual says that pressing REC during playback enters record mode; pressing REC while stopped enters record-ready mode, then PLAY starts recording.

From the performer’s perspective, the sequencer section is on the right half of the top panel, with the ribbon keyboard along the lower area. In the sequencer control cluster, the REC button is a small press button near the PLAY button and FLUX button. Use PLAY as the transport landmark: REC is in that same upper sequencer button group, not among the eight step buttons.

To record:

Press PLAY to start the sequence.
Press REC. The REC LED should indicate record mode, but I cannot verify its current LED state from static sources alone.
Play the ribbon keyboard; your performance is recorded into the synth sequence.
Press REC again to leave record mode.

Alternative: while stopped, press REC first. That puts the monotribe in record-ready mode; then press PLAY to begin recording.

## R2
Use the REC button in the Sequencer section. The manual says pressing REC during playback puts the monotribe into record mode; then anything you play on the ribbon keyboard is recorded into the synth sequence. Pressing REC while stopped puts it in record-ready mode, then pressing PLAY starts recording.

To find it non-visually, from the performer’s perspective:

Start at the right half of the top panel: this is the sequencer area.
Find the row of eight step buttons near the lower-right area.
Move upward from that row into the smaller sequencer control cluster.
The REC button is a small press button in the synth-sequence controls, near FLUX and GATE TIME. Think of it as the recording control in the cluster above the step buttons.

How to use it:

Press PLAY to start the sequence.
Press REC. The manual says the REC LED lights when record mode is active.
Play the ribbon keyboard; your performance is recorded.
Press REC again to leave record mode.

I cannot tell whether REC is currently lit or blinking from the manual or static panel references alone.

# Baseline:
## R1:
Use the REC button to record a sequence.

It is in the Sequencer section, in the upper row of synth sequence controls. In the manual’s sequencer diagram, it is control 7, positioned near the FLUX button and GATE TIME button, above the row of step buttons.

How to use it:

Press PLAY first to start the sequence, then press REC. The REC LED lights, and when you play the ribbon keyboard, your performance is recorded into the synth sequence.

Or, while stopped, press REC first. The REC LED blinks, meaning record-ready. Then press PLAY to begin recording.

Press REC again to leave record or record-ready mode; the REC LED goes dark.
