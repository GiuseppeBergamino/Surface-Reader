Noise level — structured description

Official control name: NOISE knob.

Surface role: slider
Physical type: rotary knob
Region: Synthesizer section
Group: VCO / sound-source group

Function: The NOISE knob adjusts the level of white noise mixed with the VCO output.

Signal position: Noise is generated by the noise generator, mixed with the VCO sound, then passed onward through the VCF and VCA signal path.

Musical effect: Increasing the noise level adds more white-noise content to the synth sound. This can make the sound more noisy, airy, percussive, or harsh depending on the filter and amp settings.

Possible value range: Continuous rotary amount from low to high. The manual does not give numeric minimum or maximum values.

Start in the synthesizer section.
