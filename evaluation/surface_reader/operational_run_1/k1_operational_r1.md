Prompt: Which control should I use to change the filter cutoff? Describe where it is and what it does.

Response: 

Use the CUTOFF knob in the VCF section. The manual says it adjusts the cutoff frequency of the VCF, changing the brightness of the sound: turn it left for a darker sound, and right for a brighter sound.

From the performer’s perspective, it is in the upper-middle synth area, in the filter pair. Find the two related knobs for the filter: CUTOFF is the upper knob, and PEAK is directly below it. Think: Cutoff above, Peak below.

What it does: the monotribe’s VCF is a low-pass filter, so the CUTOFF knob sets how much high-frequency content gets through. Lower cutoff makes the sound more muted or rounded; higher cutoff makes it more open and bright.
