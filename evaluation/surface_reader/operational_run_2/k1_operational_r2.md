Prompt: Which control should I use to change the filter cutoff? Describe where it is and what it does.

Response: 

Use the CUTOFF knob in the VCF / Voltage Controlled Filter section. The manual says it “adjusts the cutoff frequency of the VCF, affecting the brightness of the sound”: turn it left for a darker sound, right for a brighter sound.

From the performer’s perspective, it is in the upper-middle synth area, in the filter pair of two rotary knobs. The CUTOFF knob is the upper knob; the PEAK knob is directly below it. Think: filter pair = Cutoff above, Peak below.

What it does musically: it changes how much high-frequency content passes through the low-pass filter. Lower cutoff makes the synth more muted or rounded; higher cutoff makes it brighter or more open. I cannot determine its current setting from the manual or static panel references alone.
