Prompt: Which control should I use to increase the noise level? Describe where it is and what it does.

Response:

Use the NOISE knob. It adjusts “the level of white noise that’s mixed with the VCO output” on the monotribe.

From the performer’s perspective, it is in the upper-left synthesizer section, in the VCO group. It is a rotary knob. Use the left side of the panel as your starting landmark: in the VCO area, the NOISE knob sits near the OCTAVE selector and WAVE select switch, before the filter controls. The manual’s synth panel overview identifies it as control 3 in that section.

Turn it clockwise to increase the noise level. Musically, this adds more white noise into the oscillator signal before it goes through the filter and amplifier. I cannot tell where your physical knob is currently set from the manual or static images alone.
