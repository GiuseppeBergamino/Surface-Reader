Prompt: Which control should I use to change the oscillator octave? Describe where it is and what it does.

Response:

Use the OCTAVE selector in the VCO section. The manual says it “specifies the VCO pitch in steps of an octave”; at the 64’ setting, the ribbon keyboard range is A0–D2.

From the performer’s perspective, it is in the upper-left synthesizer area, at the start of the VCO group. It is the first control in the synthesizer section diagram, near the WAVE select switch and NOISE knob.

Tactile anchor: start at the upper-left area of the top panel. Find the oscillator/VCO control cluster. The OCTAVE selector is a discrete selector control, not a continuous tone-shaping knob: use it when you want the synth/ribbon keyboard pitch range to jump by octaves, rather than glide or fine-tune.
