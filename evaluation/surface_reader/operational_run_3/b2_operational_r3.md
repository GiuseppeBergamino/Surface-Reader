Prompt: Which control should I use to record a sequence? Describe where it is and how to use it.

Response: 

Use the REC button in the Sequencer section. It is the record control for recording a synth sequence. The manual says that pressing REC during playback puts the monotribe into record mode; then playing the ribbon keyboard records your performance. Pressing REC while stopped puts it in record-ready mode, and pressing PLAY then starts recording.

From the performer’s perspective, the sequencer area is on the right half and lower-right area of the top panel. Use the ribbon keyboard as a landmark: it is the long touch strip along the lower part of the panel. Just above that lower area is the sequencer control cluster. The REC button is a small press button in the synth sequencer controls, near PLAY, FLUX, and GATE TIME. The visual index identifies the sequencer layout diagram on manual page 13 and sequencer behavior details on page 15.

To record a synth sequence:

Press PLAY to start the sequence.
Press REC. The manual says the REC LED lights in record mode.
Play the ribbon keyboard; your performance is recorded into the synth part.
Press REC again to leave record mode.

Alternative: while stopped, press REC first. This puts the unit in record-ready mode; then press PLAY to begin recording.
