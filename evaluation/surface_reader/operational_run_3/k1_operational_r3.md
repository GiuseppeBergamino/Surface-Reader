Prompt: Which control should I use to change the filter cutoff? Describe where it is and what it does.

Response: 

Use the CUTOFF knob in the VCF section. The manual says it adjusts the filter cutoff frequency, changing the brightness of the sound: turn it left for a darker sound, and right for a brighter sound.

From the performer’s perspective, it is in the upper-middle area of the top panel, in the filter pair. Find the two related round knobs in the filter group: CUTOFF is the upper knob, and PEAK is directly below it. PEAK adds resonance or emphasis around the cutoff frequency, so the two work together as the main tone-shaping pair.
