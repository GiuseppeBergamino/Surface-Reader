Prompt: Which control should I use to change the oscillator octave? Describe where it is and what it does.

Response:

Use the OCTAVE selector. It changes the VCO pitch in octave steps. The manual says that at the 64’ setting, the ribbon keyboard’s pitch range is A0–D2.

From the performer’s perspective, it is in the upper-left area of the synthesizer section, in the VCO/oscillator group. It is the leftmost control in the top VCO area, near the WAVE select switch and above the other oscillator controls such as NOISE and RANGE. The visual index identifies manual page 10 as the top-panel synthesizer layout used for mapping these controls.

Tactile cue: find the synth controls on the left half of the top panel. The OCTAVE selector is a discrete-position selector, not a smooth sound-shaping knob. Use it when you want the whole oscillator/ribbon pitch range shifted higher or lower by octaves.
