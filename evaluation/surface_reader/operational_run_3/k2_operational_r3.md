Prompt: Which control should I use to increase the noise level? Describe where it is and what it does.

Response:

Use the NOISE knob.

It is in the Synthesizer section, in the VCO group. From the performer’s perspective, it is in the upper-left area of the top panel, near the oscillator controls. Tactilely, it is a small rotary knob; turn it clockwise to increase the noise level.

What it does: the manual says the NOISE knob adjusts the level of white noise that is mixed with the VCO output. So increasing it adds more white noise into the synth sound before it goes onward through the filter and amp.

I cannot tell its current setting from the manual or static panel photo alone.
