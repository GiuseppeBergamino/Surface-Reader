Prompt: Which control should I use to change the filter cutoff? Describe where it is and what it does.

Response: 

Use the CUTOFF knob in the VCF section.

It is in the synthesizer controls, in the upper-middle area of the panel’s main knob section: below the VCO controls, to the left of the PEAK knob, and above the LEVEL knob.

It adjusts the cutoff frequency of the VCF. Turning it left makes the sound darker; turning it right makes the sound brighter. The manual says the VCF changes the timbre by boosting or cutting frequency regions, and the monotribe uses a 12 dB/oct low-pass filter.
