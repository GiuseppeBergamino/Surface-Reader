Prompt: Which control should I use to increase the noise level? Describe where it is and what it does.

Response:

Use the NOISE knob to increase the noise level.

It is in the Synthesizer section, near the upper-left area of the main panel. In the manual’s numbered panel diagram, it is control 3, below the OCTAVE selector and left of the RANGE select switch.

The NOISE knob adjusts the level of white noise mixed with the VCO output. Turn it to the right to increase the amount of white noise in the synth sound.
