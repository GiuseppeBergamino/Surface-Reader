Prompt: Which control should I use to increase the noise level? Describe where it is and what it does.

Response:

Use the NOISE knob to increase the noise level. Turning it clockwise increases the level of white noise mixed with the VCO output.

It is in the Synthesizer section, near the upper-left area of the main panel controls. In the manual’s numbered layout it is control 3, directly associated with the VCO section, near the OCTAVE selector and WAVE select switch.

What it does: the NOISE knob “adjusts the level of white noise that’s mixed with the VCO output.” This adds more noise into the synth sound before it continues through the filter and amplifier.
