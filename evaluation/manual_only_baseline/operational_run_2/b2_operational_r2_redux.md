Prompt: Which control should I use to record a sequence? Describe where it is and how to use it.

Response: 

Use the REC button to record a synth sequence.

It is in the sequencer section, on the right side of the top panel. It is in the row of synthesizer sequence controls, between the FLUX button and the GATE TIME button, above the row of step buttons.

To use it:

Press PLAY to start the sequence.
Press REC during playback. The monotribe enters record mode, and the REC LED lights.
Play the ribbon keyboard. Your performance data is recorded into the sequence.
Press REC again to leave record mode; the REC LED goes dark.

You can also press REC while stopped to enter record-ready mode; then press PLAY to begin recording.
