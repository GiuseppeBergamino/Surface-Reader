Prompt: Which control should I use to change the filter cutoff? Describe where it is and what it does.

Response: 

Use the CUTOFF knob in the VCF section.

It is control 5 in the synthesizer section, in the filter area, near the PEAK knob.

The CUTOFF knob adjusts the cutoff frequency of the VCF, changing the brightness of the sound. Turn it left to make the sound darker; turn it right to make the sound brighter.
