Prompt: Which control should I use to record a sequence? Describe where it is and how to use it.

Response: 

Use the REC button to record a sequence.

It is in the Sequencer section, in the row of small buttons near the upper middle-right of that section. In the manual’s sequencer diagram, it is control 7, between FLUX on its left and GATE TIME on its right.

To record the synth part:

Press PLAY to start the sequence.
Press REC. The REC LED lights, and the monotribe enters record mode.
Play the ribbon keyboard; your performance data is recorded into the sequence.
Press REC again to exit record mode.

You can also press REC while stopped. The REC LED blinks for record-ready mode; then press PLAY to begin recording.
