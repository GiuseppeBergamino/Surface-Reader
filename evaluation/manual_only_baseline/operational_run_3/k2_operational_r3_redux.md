Prompt: Which control should I use to increase the noise level? Describe where it is and what it does.

Response:

Use the NOISE knob to increase the noise level.

It is in the Synthesizer section, near the upper-left area of the panel. In the manual’s numbered layout, it is control 3, below/near the VCO controls and close to the WAVE and RANGE switches.

What it does: the NOISE knob adjusts the level of white noise mixed with the VCO output. Turn it up to add more white noise to the synth sound.
